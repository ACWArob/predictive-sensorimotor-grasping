# PSG related work, round 2 (Oct 22, 2026)

Bottom line: nothing found does PSG's full loop: predict the load, check it at contact checkpoints, correct it per object in one grasp, inflate variance on surprise, learn the margin from success. Two papers each cover a piece and must be cited and contrasted: **Exp-Force** (2603.08668) and **Huang et al.** (2503.11057). All CoRL 2026 workshop paper deadlines have passed.

## Mentor's list

| Paper | What it does | Relation to PSG |
|---|---|---|
| ENPIRE, arXiv 2606.19980 (NVIDIA/CMU/Berkeley, Jun 2026) | Coding agents (Claude Code, Codex) run the whole real-world loop: reset, rollout, verify, rewrite training code. Up to 99% on dexterous tasks, 100% on pin insertion. Using 8 robots instead of 1 cut Push-T time from about 5 h to about 2 h. Uses gripper torque as a stand-in for slip. | Framing only. Its torque/force success checks are the same signals as our comparator. Several authors also organize the Agentic Robotics workshop. |
| LIFT "Never Too Late for Force", arXiv 2607.14236 (SJTU, Jul 2026) | A reactive action expert beside π0.5. 6D force goes through a causal force memory and zero-init cross-attention. Book insertion 0.6 vs 0.4, Hanoi 0.6 vs 0.2. On towel folding, the version without force did better (0.95 vs 0.825). | Reacts *after* contact; PSG predicts *before*. Best "force into a VLA" citation. Borrow zero-init cross-attention if F/T goes into MAGPIE-VLA. |
| Metis, arXiv 2607.26760 (Jul 2026) | Memory foundation model (LLM). Persistent memory state, memory attention, updates with no gradients and frozen weights. | Conceptual cousin: our per-object residual is also a gradient-free, forward-only memory update. Low risk. |
| Mirage, RSS 2024 p069 (Berkeley/GDM) | Cross-embodiment transfer by inpainting the target robot out and rendering the source robot in, plus forward-dynamics tracking. Franka→UR5: 90% on Tiger-in-Bowl vs 0% for Diffusion Policy. | Not PSG. Relevant only if MAGPIE-VLA policies trained on another arm must run on the UR5. |
| EXPO-FT, arXiv 2605.25477 (Stanford: Dong, Sadigh, Finn) | π0.5 kept training with its supervised objective; RL never backpropagates through it. A small edit actor plus a TD-trained Q picks base vs edited action. 30/30 on 8 tasks with about 19 min of online data. | Closest template for our value head (frozen base + bounded residual + Q selection). Cite it as the residual-RL design. |
| Q-Planning, arXiv 2608.21204 (Garg group, Aug 2026) | Off-policy Q on a frozen BC policy, trained on successes **and** failures. LIBERO-10 93→99%. Real robot: 40→90% and 25→80%. Success-only SFT plateaus at 55% and 30%. | **Challenges our success-only value head.** Either justify it (drops are costly) or add a failure-aware ablation. |
| LMPC, arXiv 2402.11450 (GDM, 2024) | LLM fine-tuned as a transition model for MPC over human feedback. +26.9% teaching success. | Related-work line only. Mentor says it's weak. |

## Closest to PSG (must cite)

| Paper | arXiv | Overlap |
|---|---|---|
| Exp-Force | 2603.08668 | **High.** Retrieval + VLM pre-grasp force, 0.43 N MAE, 63→87% appropriate. Open-loop: no checkpoints, no per-object online correction. Already our baseline. Note its hallucinated DeliGrasp citation politely. |
| Huang et al., *Enhancing Regrasping Efficiency Using Prior Grasping Perceptions with Soft Fingertips* | 2503.11057 | **Medium-high.** One-shot per-object force reuse for regrasping. No vision prior, no uncertainty, no learned margin. Contrast it explicitly. |
| *Robot Learning with Visual Predicted Force* (Chen, Adelson, Wu, Howe, Du) | 2610.04741 (Oct 3 2026) | **Medium, concurrent.** Predicts force visually from Fin Ray deformation and picks the action whose force matches the demos. Not a load/slip forward model. Its lead author organizes the Compositional Generalization workshop. |
| V2F (date fruit grasp force) | 2607.19804 | Vision → safe-force prior with a Hertz residual. Single domain. |
| FORTE (tactile force/slip on compliant fingers) | 2506.18960 | Sensing for the slip checkpoint. |
| Bioinspired slip control, Nat. Mach. Intell. 2025 | — | Human-motor framing. |

No robot paper found framed around the size-weight illusion or Johansson grip-load coupling (Google Scholar not yet checked).
