# PSG related work, round 3 (Nov 3, 2026)

**Novelty:** not scooped, but the claim must narrow. Beveridge & Nayar (arXiv 2610.11221, Oct 8 2026) already publish "vision prior → online per-object posterior → minimum grasp force" (GelSight, >2× less excess force). PSG's distinct pieces: a forward model checked at named control points with grip-load reflexes, a cross-grasp residual with surprise-gated variance, and all of it from wrist F/T only, with no tactile skin. Claim "neuro-inspired predict–compare–correct across grasps, from F/T only", not "per-object grasp-force adaptation".

## Upgrades for PSG, in priority order
1. **Value-head reward = success − λ·excess force.** At 97% success, a success-only reward barely varies, which is why the head added nothing. Alternatively drop the head for an analytic margin (k·σ) plus a pre-lift failure gate.
2. **Update the mass estimate during the load ramp**, within the grasp, not only between grasps. This answers the overlap with 2610.11221 and lifts first-grasp success on deceptive objects (currently 83%).
3. **Physics-informed forward model:** load = m(g+a) and Coulomb slip, with an RFF residual on top. Better extrapolation.
4. **Fast slip channel** (OptoForce vibration or a piezo) feeding the ×1.4 reflex.
5. **Add newer baselines:** a RETAF/ForceRFT-style force module, Beveridge & Nayar's posterior.

Already done today (found by the v1-vs-v2 harness): floor and clip the lift-off load innovation, and add a failure-aware residual bump after a drop.
